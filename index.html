🧳 Before You Go: Travel Tips

A few essentials to make the trip smoother, cheaper, and more respectful of local culture.

🌤️ Best Time to Visit

April–October (dry season) offers the best surf swells and clearest temple views. May–June and September are less crowded with great weather.

🚗 Getting Around

Hire a private driver for full days (~$45–60/day) between regions, and rent a scooter (~$6/day) for short local hops in Ubud or Canggu.

⛩️ Temple Etiquette

Bring or rent a sarong and sash (often included with entry). Cover shoulders/knees, and avoid entering during ceremonies if menstruating, per local custom.

🏄 Surf Safety

Book a certified instructor for the first sessions (~$35–45/hr for two), start at beginner-friendly beach breaks like Batu Bolong before Uluwatu's reef breaks.

💵 Money & Payments

Carry cash (IDR) for warungs and temple donations; cards work at villas/resorts. Negotiate politely at markets — it's expected.

📶 SIM & Connectivity

Grab a local eSIM/SIM (Telkomsel) on arrival at the airport for ~$10–15 — handy for maps, ride-hailing, and booking activities.

🗓️ Day-by-Day Itinerary

Three bases keep travel time low while covering surf, temples, and relaxation: Uluwatu/Bingin → Ubud → Seminyak/Canggu.
